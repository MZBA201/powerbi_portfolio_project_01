# Business Intelligence Case Study: FlyingWhale Airline

## Project Overview

This project analyses customer flight activity and loyalty history for FlyingWhale Airline, a fictional international airline. The objective is to understand customer travel patterns, analyse loyalty programme performance, assess customer lifetime value, and examine membership cancellations.

I developed a Power BI dashboard to analyse the available data and prepared a presentation summarising the main findings and recommendations.

## Business Questions

The project investigates the following areas:

### 1. Flight Activity Analysis
- How does flight activity vary by month and year?
- How does flight activity change throughout the year?
- How many points are redeemed for flights booked with companions?
- How are flight distance and loyalty points related?

### 2. Customer Loyalty Segmentation
- How does flight activity vary across loyalty card categories?
- How are loyalty members distributed by gender, marital status, and other demographic characteristics?
- What is the median flight distance for each loyalty card category?
- Which loyalty card category has the highest average customer lifetime value (CLV)?

### 3. Enrollment and Cancellation
- What is the average enrollment duration among cancelled members?
- Which provinces have the shortest average enrollment duration among cancelled members?
- During which months are cancellations highest?
- How does enrollment duration differ across loyalty card categories?
- What strategies could improve customer retention?

## Key Findings

The analysis of flight activity and customer loyalty data produced the following findings:

- **Flight activity:** Flights show seasonal patterns, with peaks in July and December. July has the higher peak.
- **Companion bookings:** The presentation reports that the highest points redeemed for flights booked were associated with three flights, both for individual members and for bookings involving additional passengers.
- **Loyalty segmentation:** Star is the largest loyalty category across gender, marital status, and geographic location.
- **Flight distance:** Star members have the highest median flight distance at 519 km, while Nova members have the lowest median distance.
- **Customer lifetime value:** Aurora members have the highest average CLV, followed by Nova members.
- **Membership cancellations:** The average enrollment duration among cancelled members is 15.88 months. December has the highest number of cancellations.
- **Enrollment duration:** Aurora members have the lowest average enrollment duration among the loyalty card categories.

## Recommendations

Based on the findings presented:

- Focus marketing campaigns and promotional offers on Aurora and Nova members, particularly during peak travel periods in July and December.
- Investigate why Aurora members have the lowest average enrollment duration.
- Collect feedback from existing and departing members to better understand factors associated with membership cancellation.

## Data

The case study uses two datasets:

- **Customer Flight Activity:** Flight bookings, companion bookings, distance travelled, points accumulated, and points redeemed.
- **Customer Loyalty History:** Customer demographics, loyalty card category, customer lifetime value, enrollment details, and cancellation details.

The analysis focuses on the 2017 and 2018 flight activity period where applicable.
